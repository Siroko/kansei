# room.glb

The motion-matching room's scene, authored in Blender (the .blend is kept outside this repository)
and packed by `tools/room_glb.py`. Everything in it is CC0 (public domain):

- Models from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com/a/<name>, CC0): modern_arm_chair_01, Ottoman_01,
  modern_coffee_table_01, coffee_table_round_01, side_table_01, modern_ceiling_lamp_01,
  metal_stool_02, steel_frame_shelves_01, steel_frame_shelves_03, drawer_cabinet, potted_plant_04,
  calathea_orbifolia_01, planter_pot_clay, ceramic_pot, marble_bust_01, horse_statue_01,
  bronze_whale_statue, bronze_ray_statue, concrete_cat_statue, ceramic_vase_01, ceramic_vase_04,
  throw_pillows_01. Decimated and re-textured as KTX2.
- Surfaces from ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com/a/<Id>, CC0): Concrete044A, Plaster001,
  Bricks097, Concrete028, Concrete034, Metal032, Wood066, Fabric061, Carpet016, Marble012,
  Marble016.
- The lounge's two armchairs: an MG5-style cantilever armchair modelled for the room (CC0), also
  on its own as `cantilever_armchair_mg5.glb`; its textures are baked from a model of it with
  ambientCG Leather026, Leather037, Leather033A, Scratches005 and SurfaceImperfections017 (CC0).
- Made for the room (CC0): the architecture, the sofa, tables, floor lamps, aquarium, plinths and
  parkour pieces (modelled by script in Blender), and the three abstract canvases (generated).

The glass dragon the page places on the plinth is not in this file: it is
`../stanford_dragon_pbr.glb`, "Stanford Dragon (Vrip)" by 3D graphics 101, CC-BY-NC-4.0.
