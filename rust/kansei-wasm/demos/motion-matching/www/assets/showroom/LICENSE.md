# showroom.glb

The motion-matching showroom's architecture, authored in Blender (the .blend is kept outside this
repository) and packed by `tools/room_glb.py`. Everything in it is CC0 (public domain):

- Surfaces from ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com/a/<Id>, CC0): Concrete044A, Plaster001,
  Concrete034, Metal032, Wood066.
- Made for the showroom (CC0): the hall, its windows and skylight, the plinths, pedestals, bench,
  track rails and spots (modelled by script in Blender).

The furniture the page places on the plinths is not in this file and not in this repository: it is
the owner's, loaded from the gitignored `private/` link (see the README's "The showroom").
