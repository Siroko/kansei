Kansei engine

Examples

Small scenes made with Kansei. Each one needs a browser with WebGPU. Pages marked TS run the TypeScript engine; their sources are in examples/. Pages marked Rust / WASM run Kansei's Rust core compiled to WebAssembly, the engine's active line; their sources are in rust/kansei-wasm/examples and demos, and most take the URL parameters listed in their READMEs. Where both exist, the TS page shows the Rust one's scene.

This server has no Rust/WASM builds (they come with the site build, pnpm bundle-examples, or a local scripts/build-wasm-examples.sh), so their links open kansei.graphics.

Rendering

  • Hello scene TS The smallest complete scene: a floor, a spinning box and a sphere in the stock lit material, a sun and two point lights, shadows and an orbit camera
  • Hello scene Rust / WASM The same scene on the Rust core
  • Instancing TS A 10 × 10 × 10 grid of cubes in one draw call, each cube's model matrix an instance attribute the CPU rewrites every frame
  • Instancing Rust / WASM The same grid of instanced cubes on the Rust core
  • Flowing cubes TS A swarm of textured cubes flowing on nested trigonometry, through SSAO, god rays and depth of field
  • Compute particles TS 300 000 particles moved by a compute shader and pushed around by the mouse
  • Flora TS The compute particles page under its older name; the two files are identical
  • Transparent and transmissive TS Regression page: two transparent boxes orbit a transmissive sphere over an opaque floor, and all four must draw every frame (?check=1 logs PASS/FAIL)
  • Texture bindings TS Every binding kind a material or compute pass can declare (3D, cube, 2D array, storage, depth through a comparison sampler, an external buffer), each read back and marked PASS or FAIL

Lighting and shadows

  • Shadow casters TS The renderer's directional shadow map drawn through each material's own vertex shader: instanced cubes off their origin and an alpha-tested card whose shadow keeps its holes
  • Cascaded shadows TS 4 000 instanced trees under a low sun, shadowed by four 2048² cascades with contact-hardening penumbrae, culled per cascade on the GPU
  • Cascaded shadows Rust / WASM The same forest on the Rust core, through TAA
  • Spot lights TS A car's headlights in a night forest: photometric spot lights with contact-hardening shadows from an atlas, shaded through clustered light lists and scattered in the fog
  • Spot lights Rust / WASM The same night forest on the Rust core

Global illumination and ray tracing

  • Path-traced GI TS A Cornell box with the Stanford dragon lit by the hybrid path tracer over the raster GBuffer; ?reference=1 traces camera rays and accumulates
  • Path-traced GI with trails TS The path-traced Cornell box with compute-driven trail particles, path traced too, and a glass model
  • Voxel tracer TS The path-traced Cornell box with a switch to tracing a voxelization of the scene instead of its BVH
  • Path tracer: dragon Rust / WASM A Cornell box with a glass Stanford dragon, path traced on the GPU against a BVH
  • Voxel GI TS A Cornell box under one downlight to compare GI methods: screen-space GI, or voxel GI with its distance field, probes and ray-traced reflections
  • GI box Rust / WASM The same room on the Rust core, lit by voxel GI with screen-space GI for contact detail
  • Outdoor GI TS A forest valley 1.3 km across under a low sun, with voxel GI in a clipmap round the camera
  • Outdoor GI Rust / WASM The same valley on the Rust core
  • Voxel GI on particles TS An SPH fluid dam-breaks in an open-topped Cornell room, its particles splatted into a voxel volume and cone traced for their light
  • Voxel GI on particles Rust / WASM The Rust demo: particles raining into a lightbox, glowing ones lighting the rest through cone-traced voxel GI
  • Voxel GI, ray traced Rust / WASM The same lightbox with some particles turned to mirrors and glass, their rays walking the fluid's neighbour grid

Atmosphere and fog

  • Sky atmosphere TS A physically based sky from noon to dusk: Hillaire's LUTs, aerial perspective on distant hills, sky-lit surfaces and reflections of its prefiltered cubemap
  • Sky atmosphere Rust / WASM The same sky on the Rust core, with volumetric clouds
  • Volumetric fog TS A grove of pillars under a low sun: froxel fog with shafts through the directional shadow, a lamp's cube shadow and a dim sky
  • Volumetric fog Rust / WASM The same grove on the Rust core
  • Curl volumetric TS Curl-noise particle trails through volumetric fog, in a Cornell box or over a scrolling noise terrain
  • Particles in fog TS 4 096 trail particles in a Cornell box lit by a point light, through volumetric fog and post-processing

Post-processing

  • Post-processing TS Spheres in volumetric fog with shadows and a filmic tone map, plus path-traced GI, bloom, depth of field and colour grading to switch on
  • Film look TS A dusk path in physical light units through fog, bloom and the tone mapper: EV100 exposure, filmic curves, vignette and grain
  • Film look Rust / WASM The same dusk path on the Rust core
  • Temporal AA TS Thin trunks, power lines, 12 000 swaying grass cards and a passing car: motion vectors and temporal anti-aliasing on a single-sample GBuffer
  • Temporal AA Rust / WASM The same scene on the Rust core, optionally upscaled and motion blurred
  • DoF terrain TS A scrolling field of noise-driven columns with SSAO and a physical lens: two-layer depth of field and bokeh
  • Depth of field Rust / WASM Physical depth of field on the Rust core: trunks, leaf cards and strings of lights through a camera lens

Visibility, LOD and reflections

  • Occlusion culling TS 60 000 spruces in three LODs, culled on the GPU per view by frustum, LOD band and, for the camera, a two-phase depth pyramid
  • Occlusion culling Rust / WASM The same forest on the Rust core
  • Impostors TS A lake ringed by 40 000 spruces whose far LOD is an octahedral impostor baked at start-up, mirrored in the water
  • Impostors Rust / WASM The same lake and forest on the Rust core
  • Cluster LOD TS A field of rocks drawn three ways: cluster LOD cut per cluster on the GPU, discrete LODs from the same graph, or the full mesh
  • Cluster LOD Rust / WASM The same field of rocks on the Rust core
  • Planar reflection TS A lake at dusk mirroring the far shore, a lit cottage and a searchlight's beam in the mist: a mirrored view with an oblique clip plane, rippled and blurred by roughness
  • Planar reflection Rust / WASM The same lake on the Rust core, with a screen-space mirror to compare

Fluid

  • Fluid TS A GPU particle fluid you can stir with the mouse, with a nozzle that pours more in
  • Fluid Rust / WASM 50 000 SPH particles meshed by marching cubes and refracted, on the Rust core
  • Lake TS Water held by a shaped bed and shore, stirred by a mill wheel's paddles, its surface drawn from a surface field
  • Lake Rust / WASM The Rust demo: a lake beside a course of boxes, with a water cannon and a mill, cascaded shadows and TAA
  • Fluid text TS 2D fluid particles that gather into letters
  • Fluid clock Rust / WASM The time of day formed out of an SPH fluid, its digits pulling particles into extruded glyphs
  • Steering text Rust / WASM About 1 600 words flocking in 3D as boids, their letters trailing on distance constraints

Animation

  • Skinning TS A ring of procedurally rigged tentacles skinned in their materials' vertex shaders, casting bending shadows
  • Motion matching TS A character controller on the TS motion-matching runtime over a course of boxes; needs a local animation pack (none ships), and shows the course alone without one
  • Motion matching, live External Walk, run, jump and parkour over obstacles, plus a fluid lake to wade through, on raggare.kansei.graphics

Loaders, text and tools

  • KTX2 textures TS ETC1S and UASTC textures and a glTF through KHR_texture_basisu, transcoded to the best format this device samples, with sizes and timings
  • KTX2 textures Rust / WASM The same textures transcoded by the Rust core
  • MSDF text TS Signed-distance-field text, animated by a compute shader
  • Video texture TS The webcam as an external video texture across 250 stacked instanced planes (asks for camera access)
  • Helpers TS Stock geometries (heightfield, spruce, icosphere, cylinder), debug boxes, a follow camera and the web runtime
