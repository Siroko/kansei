Kansei engine

Examples

Small scenes made with Kansei. Each one needs a browser with WebGPU. The sources of the TypeScript examples are in examples/ on GitHub.

  • Path-traced GI A Cornell box lit by hybrid path-traced global illumination
  • Post-processing Volumetric fog with shadows, plus bloom, depth of field and colour grading to switch on
  • DoF terrain A scrolling field of noise-driven columns with SSAO and depth of field
  • Fluid A GPU particle fluid you can stir with the mouse
  • Fluid text Fluid particles that gather into letters
  • MSDF text Signed-distance-field text, animated by a compute shader
  • Motion matching A character controller with motion matching: walk, run, jump and parkour over obstacles, plus a fluid lake to wade through

Rust / WASM

Kansei's Rust core, compiled to WebAssembly: the engine's active line. Their sources are in rust/kansei-wasm/examples on GitHub; most take URL parameters, listed at the top of each one's src/lib.rs.

  • Voxel GI on particles Particles raining into a lightbox, glowing ones lighting the rest through cone-traced voxel GI
  • Voxel GI, ray traced The same lightbox with some particles turned to mirrors and glass, their rays walking the fluid's neighbour grid
  • Path tracer A Cornell box with the Stanford dragon, path traced on the GPU against a BVH
  • GI box A Cornell box under one downlight, with and without screen-space global illumination
  • Sky atmosphere A physically based sky with aerial perspective, from noon to dusk over hills and spruces
  • Volumetric fog Froxel fog with sun shafts through a grove of pillars and shadowed point lamps
  • Film look A dusk scene through the full HDR chain: fog, bloom, exposure, filmic curve, grade and grain
  • Depth of field Physical depth of field through a camera lens, with bokeh on strings of lights
  • Planar reflection A still lake at dusk mirroring a treeline, a lit cottage and the sky
  • Spot lights A car's headlights in a forest at night, shadowed on the ground and in the fog
  • Cascaded shadows A forest under a low sun, four stable cascades with contact-hardening penumbrae
  • Occlusion culling 60 000 spruces on hilly terrain, culled on the GPU per view, by LOD band and by occlusion
  • Impostors A forest ringing a lake, far trees drawn as octahedral impostors baked at start-up
  • Cluster LOD A field of rocks, each one's detail picked per cluster every frame
  • Temporal AA Thin trunks, power lines, swaying grass and a passing car under temporal anti-aliasing
  • Fluid A GPU particle fluid with a marching-cubes surface
  • Fluid clock Fluid particles that gather into the digits of a clock
  • Steering text Particles steering into words
  • Motion matching The motion-matching course and lake without the character: its animation packs are private (the full demo is linked above)
  • KTX2 textures Basis Universal textures, transcoded to the best format this device samples
  • Post-processing A first scene through the GBuffer and bloom
  • Shadow scene Directional and point-light shadows
  • Lit scene A box and a sphere under lights
  • Instanced cubes A field of instanced cubes
  • Spinning box The smallest Kansei scene
